## Room visualizer

Homepage hero paints the wall of `public/room-bg.jpg` using `public/room-wall-mask.png`
(alpha mask, wall = opaque) with multiply + color blend layers. The mask is generated by
`node scripts/generate-wall-mask.mjs` (flood-fill segmentation with hand-traced sofa
contour). If the room photo ever changes, re-run that script and re-tune its seeds and
exclusion zones.
